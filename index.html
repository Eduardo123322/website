TheBusinessLab11 Publisher

A private desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @thebusinesslab11 to publish their own original short videos (animated stories about money and small business) through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

The owner signs in with TikTok, chooses the caption and privacy for each video, and the app posts only after the owner's explicit action. It is not offered to other users.
