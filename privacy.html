TheBusinessLab11 Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

  1. Data we access. After you authorize with TikTok Login, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and the posting options TikTok returns for your account.
  2. How we use it. Only to upload and publish the videos you choose to your own account and to show you the result.
  3. Storage. Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in private storage controlled by the account owner. They are never shared, sold or logged.
  4. Sharing. No data is shared with third parties other than TikTok itself, which receives the videos you publish.
  5. Deletion. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); the stored tokens are then deleted.
  6. Contact. Through the TikTok profile https://www.tiktok.com/@thebusinesslab11.
